The House

Warm brick, timber and light on Malviya Marg.

Croft House stands at K-7, Malviya Marg, in the middle of Jaipur’s C Scheme. The address is a layered one: a glass-walled veranda under a slatted timber ceiling, a rough stone wall opening onto a courtyard gathered around a lamp-lit tree, and interiors of exposed brick, chesterfield leather and terracotta plaster.

Seating moves from tufted booth nooks to a long bar counter and garden booths set into paved stone. Light does the rest — sharp and bright through the veranda glass by day, low and golden after dusk.
